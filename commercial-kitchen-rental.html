Common questions about renting

What does "commercial kitchen rental" include?+

Your membership includes access to a fully licensed commercial kitchen with convection ovens, prep stations, induction burners, dry storage, and utilities. Higher-tier packages also include cold storage. Free permit assistance is included with every package.

Do I need permits to use the kitchen?+

Yes — you'll need a City of Port Coquitlam business license and Fraser Health food safety approval. But don't worry, most new members don't have these yet. We help you get them as part of your membership, and most people are approved within 6–8 weeks.

How is this different from renting a restaurant kitchen?+

Unlike renting a restaurant kitchen (which may have limited availability and no support), Sincerely Kitchen is purpose-built for food entrepreneurs. You get 24/7 access, your own storage, flexible scheduling, free permit assistance, and a community of fellow food business owners.

How long is the commitment?+

All memberships are 6-month agreements that automatically renew. New members can start with our Starter 10 package ($490/mo) for their first 3 months, then upgrade. You can change your package at renewal.

Can I visit the kitchen before signing up?+

Absolutely. We encourage it. Book a free 45-minute kitchen tour — we'll show you around, answer questions, and talk about your plans. No commitment required.

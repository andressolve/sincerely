What to expect on your tour

Meet us

You'll meet Maria or Andres. We'll ask about your food business ideas and where you are in your journey. No wrong answers.

~10 minutes

See the space

Walk through the full kitchen — prep stations, ovens, induction burners, storage areas. Get a feel for the workflow and the vibe.

~20 minutes

Ask anything

Permits, pricing, scheduling, what to expect — we'll answer every question. We'll also recommend the right package based on your needs.

~15 minutes

What happens next

1

Maria reads it

Every application is read personally — what you make, where you're headed, and when you'd need the kitchen.

2

We look at fit, not just timing

Does the business make sense here, are you someone this kitchen can genuinely help, and does your schedule work alongside the members already in the space.

3

If it's a fit, we talk

Maria will reach out to set up a conversation. If the timing isn't right yet, you stay on the list and we'll come back to you.

You don't have to wait to see the space. Book a free 45-minute tour whenever you like — being on the list isn't a prerequisite, and visiting isn't a commitment.

What makes a good fit

🍳
The business Something this kitchen is actually built to make well.

The person Someone ready to work, and easy to share a kitchen with.
🕔
The schedule Hours that work alongside the members already here.

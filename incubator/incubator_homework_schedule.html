Homework Schedule & Submission Guide

How to submit

  1. Every member has a private folder inside the shared "The Incubator — Cohort 1" Drive folder, shared with just you and Maria.
  2. Name each file: Week 01 - Business Concept (use the week number and deliverable name from the table below — zero-padded so files sort correctly).
  3. Homework is due every Wednesday by 11:59 p.m., ahead of that Saturday's session — this gives Maria time to review before you meet.
  4. Longer gaps between sessions (the Nov 7 skip, the Dec–Jan holiday break) just mean more time to work — the Wednesday-before-the-next-session rule always applies.

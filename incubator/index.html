Week 5 · Oct 3

Production Costing

What does it actually cost to make one? Using the Pricing Calculator, you'll turn your standardized recipes into real numbers — including the real insurance premium you requested last week, instead of guessing. We keep two kinds of cost apart: your variable cost (ingredients + packaging — the true floor, never sell below it) and your fixed costs (kitchen, overhead, insurance, and paying yourself — a monthly nut you cover with volume, not a cost per unit). The trap almost everyone falls into is dividing fixed costs into each unit and scaring themselves off with a "$9 cookie." Instead we find your contribution (price − cost to make one) and your break-even — how many you need to sell a month to get profitable. Overhead becomes a target you grow into, not a verdict.

Every launch product costed and saved in the Pricing Calculator — variable cost, contribution, and break-even for each

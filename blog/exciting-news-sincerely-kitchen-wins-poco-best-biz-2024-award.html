Sincerely Kitchen Wins "PoCo Best Biz 2024" Award!

April 17, 2024 · 1 min read

← Back to Blog

We are thrilled to announce that Sincerely Kitchen, our shared commercial kitchen in Port Coquitlam, has been recognized with the prestigious "Poco Best Biz 2024" award for best small business. This accolade is not just an award; it's a testament to the vibrant community of innovative food entrepreneurs that call our kitchen home.

A Space That Nurtures Success

At Sincerely Kitchen, our mission has always been to remove the barriers that new food business entrepreneurs face when starting out. Our fully equipped kitchen provides a cost-effective, flexible, and supportive environment that allows your culinary concepts to flourish without the burden of high startup costs.

Why Choose Sincerely Kitchen?

  • Turnkey Kitchen Solutions: From state-of-the-art equipment to health and safety compliance, everything you need is at your fingertips.
  • Flexibility to Grow: We offer various usage plans that scale with your business. Start small and grow big with us!
  • Community and Networking: Join a community of like-minded entrepreneurs. Our kitchen is not just a facility—it's a networking hub that fosters collaboration and innovation.
  • Expert Support: With workshops, mentoring, and support from industry experts, we ensure that you have the knowledge and skills to succeed.

Join Our Award-Winning Kitchen

Our recognition as the best small business in Port Coquitlam is exciting, but what truly drives us is seeing our clients succeed. Are you ready to start your food business with less risk and more support? Sincerely Kitchen is here to make that happen.

Contact us today to schedule a tour or to discuss how we can support your business dreams. Let's cook up success together!

The Program

This isn't just kitchen access.
It's a complete business education.

Most food entrepreneurs figure it out the hard way — spending months navigating permits, pricing mistakes, and retail rejections alone. The Incubator shortcuts all of that. Every module builds on the last, and Maria is with you every step of the way.

How It's Structured

Two phases. One cohesive journey.

Sep – Nov · Weeks 1–13

Pre-Incubation

Education only. Get Fraser Health approved, legally registered, branded, and sales-ready before you step into the kitchen.

$350/month
Dec – Feb · Weeks 14–24

Incubation

Education + commercial kitchen access. First production runs, first sales, retail buyer introductions, and a live graduation showcase.

$700/month
Graduation Day

By the end, you'll have all of this.

  • Fraser Health approval (market + retail)
  • CFIA-compliant product labels
  • Registered business with insurance
  • Full brand kit + product photography
  • Standardized recipes + production costing
  • Sell sheet and retail pitch deck
  • Instagram sales channel active
  • Farmers market sales underway
  • Retail buyer introductions
  • Unit economics: does your business work?
  • Online sales channel set up
  • Live pitch to buyers at graduation
Is This For You?

Who this is for — and who it isn't.

Great fit ✓
  • Career changer entering food full-time
  • Have a product idea or informal sales
  • Can commit to Saturdays for 6 months
  • Want structure, coaching, and a cohort
  • No prior business experience needed
Not the right fit
  • Already have an active food business
  • Looking for kitchen access only
  • Can't consistently commit to Saturdays
  • Already Fraser Health approved

If you already have an established business, our standard memberships are a better fit.

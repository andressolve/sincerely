The permit process, demystified

We know this is the part that scares people. Here's exactly what's involved — and how we help at every step.

Step 1

City of Port Coquitlam Business License

A license to operate a food business in PoCo.

What you need:

Your business name, business type, and the kitchen address (ours — 551 Sherling Pl).

How we help:

We provide the kitchen address, confirm zoning compliance, and review your application before you submit.

⏱ Usually 1–2 weeks
Step 2

Fraser Health Approval

A food safety permit from the local health authority.

What you need:

A completed food safety plan, your FoodSafe certificate (Level 1 minimum), and a site inspection.

How we help:

We help prepare your food safety plan, ensure documentation is complete, and prepare you for the inspection. Our kitchen is already Fraser Health approved.

⏱ 2–4 weeks after license
Step 3

You're Approved!

You're now a licensed food business in British Columbia. Time to start producing.

Total timeline:

Most members are fully approved in 4–6 weeks.

Cost to you:

$0 for our assistance. You only pay the government application fees.

🎉 Total: 4–6 weeks

Still have questions?

Do I need any experience to join?+

No. Many of our members are first-time food entrepreneurs. If you can make your product at home, you can make it here. We'll help you figure out the rest.

How long is the commitment?+

Our Starter 10 package is 3 months. After that, memberships are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or leave with 30 days notice.

What if I don't have my permits yet?+

That's totally fine — most new members don't. We help you get them as part of your membership. Most people are approved within 4–6 weeks.

Do I need my own equipment?+

Our kitchen is fully equipped with commercial ovens, prep stations, induction burners, and storage. You may want to bring specialized small tools or baking pans specific to your products, but the major equipment is all here.

Can I visit before deciding?+

Absolutely. Book a free 45-minute kitchen tour. We'll show you around, answer questions, and talk about your plans. No commitment required.

What hours can I use the kitchen?+

The kitchen is available 24/7. You get key fob access and can book your preferred hours through our online scheduling system. Early morning bakers, late night producers — we accommodate everyone.

Do you help with selling my products?+

We provide resources like our Product Pricing Calculator and blog content on topics like wholesale sell sheets and menu strategy. We also connect members with each other — our community is great at sharing tips and making introductions to retailers and market organizers.

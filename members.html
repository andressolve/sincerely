The people behind the food.

Every business here started with someone who had a dream and took the first step. Here are some of their stories.

🧀 Vegan Cheesemaker

Dianna Yale

Dee's Nutty Cheese — Handcrafted Vegan Cashew Cheese
"Sincerely Kitchen gave me the space and the confidence to turn my passion into a real business. Maria walked me through every step of the permit process — I couldn't have done it without this community."

Dee started making cashew-based vegan cheese at home and quickly outgrew her kitchen. At Sincerely Kitchen, she developed her signature flavours — Garlic & Herb, Smoky Chipotle, Dill & Chive — and now sells at farmers markets and local retailers across the Lower Mainland.

Samaya Delights

Samaya Delights — Baklava & Middle Eastern Pastries
"I've been with Sincerely Kitchen since the very beginning. This place isn't just a kitchen — it's my second home. Maria and the community here have been like family."

Samaya Delights is one of our original members — over 12 years and counting. She's built a thriving one-woman operation supplying handmade baklava to restaurants, cafes, and major retail stores including Whole Foods, Pomme, IGA, Greens, Save-On-Foods, Loblaws, and Nesters Market. Her dedication to her craft and her presence in this community is what Sincerely Kitchen is all about.

🌮 Restaurant & Tacos

Cachito Mio

Cachito Mio — Authentic Mexican Cuisine
"We started at Sincerely Kitchen selling Mexican insect salts — sal de chapulín. Maria believed in us from day one. When we wanted to grow, she helped us convert a storage space into our first taco shop."

Cachito Mio's journey is one of our favourite success stories. They started small, selling artisanal Mexican ingredients, then grew into Uber Eats delivery, and eventually opened a taco shop right here with Sincerely Kitchen's support. After a year of operating, they opened a second location and now also run a food truck. From insect salts to two restaurants and a food truck — that's the kind of growth we love to see.

Members who've graduated

Some of our proudest moments are when members outgrow us. These businesses started right here and have moved on to their own commercial spaces.

Monique Lisek

YVR Cookie

What started as a home baking hobby grew into one of the Lower Mainland's most-loved cookie brands. Monique built her following (45K+ on Instagram) while producing at Sincerely Kitchen, and eventually opened her own bakery in Maple Ridge.

Chae & Jimmy Kim

Earthling Foods

This brother-and-sister team developed their Korean dacaron and gluten-free treats at Sincerely Kitchen. They've since landed shelf space at T&T Supermarket and H-Mart, and now operate from their own dedicated production facility.

Amy Shaba

AbracaShaba Focacceria

Amy perfected her focaccia recipes at Sincerely Kitchen, building a loyal following at farmers markets. She's since opened her own café and bakery in Maple Ridge — a full brick-and-mortar born from a shared kitchen dream.

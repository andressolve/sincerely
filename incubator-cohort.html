A few common questions

Do I need a product already?

You should have at least a general food product in mind. It doesn't need to be fully developed — Week 1 of the program is dedicated to sharpening your concept before you spend money on it.

Is $3,150 all-in?

The program fee covers all 24 sessions, Maria's monthly 1:1 coaching, and kitchen access during the incubation phase. Your own business expenses — ingredients, packaging, business registration — are separate.

What's the time commitment beyond Saturdays?

Expect 2–4 hours per week outside of sessions — mostly completing that week's deliverable. During the incubation phase (Dec–Feb) you'll also schedule your own kitchen production time.

Come meet us.

90 minutes at the kitchen. Free. No commitment. You'll leave with a clear picture of whether this is the right next step for you.
